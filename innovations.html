INNOVATIONS

Necessity is the mother of invention.

Tom Blake, Hobie Alter, George Greenough… these guys are among my influences because they were not only great watermen, but were (GG still is!) also prodigious inventors. For them, making the tools of the trade was as rewarding as plying the trade itself. I feel the same way. If there's something I imagine will make my time on the water more fun, fast or elegant, I'll want to make it.

Fin Carrier

The Fin Carrier is for us longboard nerds. Not only will it hold your fin collection but it will also provide easy access to fin hardware, wax, tools and anything else essential for prepping your favorite log in the parking lot of your home break.

Spare Tire Paddle

OC1 and OC2 paddlers should always carry a spare paddle, but we often don't. It's often too much of a hassle to awkwardly strap a heavy extra paddle to the rear iako. And when paddlers pay big bucks to sand the paint off their canoes to save a pound or two, it feels wrong to add that weight right back on with the extra paddle. The solution is an incredibly light (under 12oz) and small spare paddle that is only there for an emergency, just like the spare tire in your car. The spare tire paddle also comes with lightweight and low-profile mounts so you can easily click the paddle in place on the canoe deck behind the seat, or even rig a full size paddle if need be. In this position it doesn't create undue windage or extra weight on the ama.

GPS Mounts

If you own a fancy GPS watch, you know how annoying it is to wear it on your wrist, where you can't see it easily while paddling, or rigging it on the front iako where you can't reach it! These lightweight mounts allow you to easily rig your watch where you can see it clearly and reach it easily.
