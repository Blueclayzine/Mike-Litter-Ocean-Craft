WORKSHOP

Come lend a hand.

As a kid I bought my first custom surfboard from the late Glenn Kennedy. He humored me and asked what I wanted. He then proceeded to make what he knew I needed. That kicked off a decades-long relationship between surfer and shaper that left a lasting impression. Now, as the shaper, the fun part is working with other ocean lovers to create their perfect piece of equipment.

Come by the shop. We'll make custom templates and talk design. And once we create a paddle, hand plane or board, for as long as I'm doing this, you're welcome to bring it back for re-finishing, repair or modification. I can't guarantee I'll be able to do all the upkeep for free, but in the spirit of keeping our creations in service for as long as possible, I'd sure like to.
