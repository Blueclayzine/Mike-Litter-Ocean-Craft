OC1

A good one-man paddle easily catches the water and exits cleanly out the back. It also must strike the perfect balance between weight, strength and stiffness. The OC1-specific blades weigh between 15oz and 18 oz depending on length, blade width, shaft circumference and timber choice. I can make them stiff for big surf runs or extremely lively for long distances in calmer water.
