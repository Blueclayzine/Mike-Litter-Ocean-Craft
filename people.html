PEOPLE

Team

Photo of Dan Hammer

Dan Hammer

Dan paddles really fast and has a competitive outrigger resume too long to list. But most importantly, Dan paddles because he loves it. He's known for secret training so even if you haven't seen him at practice in a while, watch out! He was the first of my training partners who committed to using my all-wood paddles full time. Thanks to Dan I've refined my designs to be both fast and extra durable. He's also the only person known to have broken one of my paddles, which allowed me to confirm the theory that an all-wood paddle is much more repairable than a composite one. Dan is an incredible family man, environmentalist and friend. I'm proud to make his paddles.

Photo of Poppy Nelson-Smith

Poppy Nelson-Smith

Poppy is a rising star in International junior paddling. She placed 4th at the 2024 IVF Worlds in the girls distance event and 3rd in the V6 event. She also set a course record for the Wild Buffalo Relay junior mixed division and won the open mixed division as well. She prefers a traditional rudderless canoe, which pairs nicely with her MLOC all-wood paddles. On top of being a phenomenal paddler, Poppy is a keen student whose favorite subjects are Chemistry and Exotic Animal Husbandry! While most sixteen year olds are maxed out managing their instagram feed, Poppy manages school, two jobs, and a rigorous training routine. Despite all of that, she still found time to organize a Wild Buffalo Relay campaign on her own—setting up transportation, an escort boat and canoes, while also raising over $1,000 for Save SF Bay. If anyone is looking to hire a CEO, Poppy will be available in a few years!

Photo of Sami Freeman and Matt Scanlan

Sami Freeman and Matt Scanlan

Matt and Sami are a local Ocean Beach couple who's love for each other is fed by time together in the ocean. When they are not busy parenting their two little ones, these two aquatic creatures can be found surfing, bodysurfing and cold-plunging together. Matt is a fine furniture maker and Sami is a performing artist and music composer. The joy they bring to the beach and the craftsmanship they value in their careers embodies the spirit of MLOC.

Photo of Max Lohr

Max Lohr

Max is pure stoke. He cruises the neighborhood with a big smile and a warm greeting for all. Max has an artist's eye and an old soul. He loves classic surfboards and all things design. My favorite turbo lunch tray design was born out of a template we created together. When he's not riding and styling on a soulful surfboard out at Ocean Beach, he can be found hunting tubes in the shore break on his turbo lunch tray. Max is also responsible for this beautiful website!
